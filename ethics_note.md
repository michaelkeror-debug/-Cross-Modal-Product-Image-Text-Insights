# SokoniHub multimodal ethics note (teaching)

## Who is in the photos
Lab fixtures use sidecar tags, not a crawl of faces. If tags include person/face/child, needs_privacy_review is true.

## Whose language
Tiny English VOCAB drops Kenyan product words. vocab_gaps.json lists them. We will not force every title into US-English to chase cosine.

## Generated pixels
Campaign still-lifes live in outputs/ with not_a_photo_of_the_sku. They never authenticate NK-99.

## Review path
Flags go to sellers/moderators. No silent title rewrite. No auto-takedown on bag-of-words alone.
